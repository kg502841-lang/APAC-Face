Clinical Rationale

Why Diverse Facial Landmark Detection Matters

Facial landmark models are used to understand the geometric structure of a face. Differences in facial morphology across populations can affect landmark localization. Faceify's APAC-focused research evaluates whether models maintain consistent localization across diverse facial structures.

More Inclusive AI

Overcoming historical dataset bias by actively benchmarking models against diverse facial shapes, nasal bridges, and contour profiles.

Better Landmark Localization

Sub-pixel precision on challenging anatomical regions such as lower eyelid contours, philtrum width, and jawline curvature.

More Consistent Analysis

Eliminates landmark jitter and localization drift between varying lighting, head orientations, and skin tones.

Pre-Consultation Visualization

Provides objective spatial references so prospective patients and clinicians can discuss aesthetic options transparently.

How It Works

Streamlined 4-step pipeline from client capture to geometric benchmark.

01

Upload / Camera

High-definition webcam stream or local photo input processed entirely in memory.

02

Face Detection

Ultra-fast single-shot detector identifies face region and bounds the region of interest.

03

Landmark Localization

MediaPipe regresses a 478-point mesh; the trained APAC-Face network regresses 98 WFLW landmarks.

04

Dataset Comparison

Euclidean error and NME computed against real manual annotations whenever they exist.

Research Initiative

Building a More Inclusive Landmark Model

Exploring morphological variations across East Asia, South Asia, and Southeast Asia to engineer robust landmark localization.

East Asia

Evaluates landmark fidelity on varied epicanthic fold geometry, flatter nasal dorsum profiles, and broader zygomatic contours.

Morphology Benchmarked

South Asia

Benchmarking higher nasal root prominence, deeper orbital vaults, and diverse melanin gradations under natural lighting conditions.

Southeast Asia

Focuses on distinctive alar base widths, varied facial roundness, and cultural ornamentation or makeup adaptation.
