Landmark labelling

Place 68 points on each face by hand. Every face starts from the same average face shape, never from a model's prediction, so the labels can fairly test every model, ours included.

Starting from MediaPipe's points can nudge you towards keeping them, which helps MediaPipe, never APAC-Face. So a win for APAC-Face on these labels is a conservative result. Still check and move every point that is not exactly right. Each saved face records which start it used.

How to place the points

  • Sides are the image's left and right, as you see it on screen.
  • Jaw (1–17): along the visible edge of the face, from about eye level next to the ear down to the chin (point 9 is the chin tip) and up the other side. Spread the points evenly.
  • Eyebrows (18–27): along the upper edge of each brow, outer to inner on the left, inner to outer on the right.
  • Nose (28–36): 28–31 down the bridge to the tip; 32–36 along the bottom of the nostrils, left to right, 34 under the tip.
  • Eyes (37–48): exact eye corners first, then the lid edges where lid meets eyeball.
  • Mouth (49–68): 49 and 55 are the outer mouth corners; 49–60 follow the outer lip edge, 61–68 the inner edge where the lips meet.
  • Hidden points: place your best estimate and tick "hidden". Skip a face only if you cannot label it at all.
